# Bookmarks

> **Sign in to use this.** Bookmarks are per-user and aren't visible to other users or anonymous visitors.

Bookmarks let you save frequently-visited diagrams, packages, and elements for quick return. Useful when you're deep in review and need to keep a short list of "things to come back to".

![Bookmarks](/guide/bookmarks.png)

## Adding a bookmark

On any diagram, package, or element detail page, click the **Bookmark** button in the top-right of the page header. The button toggles state — filled when bookmarked, outlined when not. Colour changes to the primary accent.

There's no batch-bookmark action — each item is bookmarked individually.

## Where bookmarks show up

- **Dashboard → Discover tab**: a **Bookmarks** section above the Recent Visits list, showing up to five recent bookmarks with deep links.
- **`/bookmarks`**: the full bookmarks page. Entries are grouped by set, sorted by most recently bookmarked.
- **Sidebar**: a **Bookmarks** nav item (signed-in users only). Shows a counter badge with the total.

## Managing bookmarks

On `/bookmarks`:

- **Click an entry** to navigate to it.
- **Click the remove (×) icon** to un-bookmark. No confirmation — bookmarks are non-destructive, so deleting one just removes a pointer.
- **Filter by set** — dropdown at the top of the page.

## What's not here

- **No shared bookmarks.** Bookmarks are per-user by design. If you want to share "these five diagrams are the important ones", use **Ask AI context** (which can include a list of specific entities) or just paste the URLs.
- **No bookmark folders.** Flat list per user; group by set is the only organisation layer.
- **No anonymous bookmarks.** Without a user identity, bookmarks can't persist — the sidebar item and page are hidden for anonymous visitors.
