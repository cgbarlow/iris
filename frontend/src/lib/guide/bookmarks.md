# Bookmarks

Bookmarks let signed-in users save frequently-visited diagrams, packages, and elements for quick access.

![Bookmarks](/guide/bookmarks.png)

## Adding a bookmark

On any diagram, package, or element detail page, click the bookmark icon in the top-right. The bookmark is saved per user (not globally) and persists across sessions.

## Managing bookmarks

The Bookmarks list at `/bookmarks` shows all of your saved entities grouped by type. Click an entry to jump to it; click the remove icon to un-bookmark.

## Anonymous note

Bookmarks require a signed-in user. The Bookmarks menu item and `/bookmarks` page are hidden for anonymous visitors.
